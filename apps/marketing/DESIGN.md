# Design System: SILKDEV

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Vantage"**

SILKDEV does not ask for permission. The site speaks from a position of advantage: a studio that watched the market commoditize and repositioned itself above the crowd, selling the shovels. Every surface is dark, deliberate, and sharp — no pastel optimism, no soft apology, no decorative warmth. The void background is the default state; violet signal and cyan reflex appear only where they mean something: an action, a live product, a decision point. Typography carries the confidence — Drystick's blocky uppercase display is the studio's voice, loud and unhurried — while Noto Sans body text stays clinical and readable underneath. Interaction is immediate: things press, glow on hover, and never linger.

The system is layered, not shadowed. Depth comes from tonal surfaces and blurred glow blobs, not box shadows.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Void-first: near-black backgrounds everywhere, surfaces slightly lighter, borders barely visible
- Signal rarity: violet/cyan accents are scarce and purposeful, never decorative wallpaper
- Uppercase voice: display type and labels shout in caps; body copy stays quiet and small
- Immediate interaction: press feedback (scale 0.97), ease-out motion, hover glows
- Craft details: custom cursor (dot + ring), grid-pattern overlays, glass surfaces

## Colors

A cold, high-contrast palette: deep void neutrals against two cold accents (violet signal and cyan reflex). No warm colors in the system.

### Primary
- **Signal Violet** (#5a52e0): The single action color (white text on it = 5.65:1, WCAG AA). Primary buttons, active nav states, focus rings, cursor dot. Hover deepens to #5a52e0.

### Secondary
- **Cyan Reflex** (#00d9ff): The "future" color — **glows, borders, gradients only**. Text-safe light cyan **#66e3ff** (~13:1 on void) is used wherever cyan must be read as text (card taglines, icons). Used for the live-product accent (SILKGUILD), service/tech accents, and moments that point forward. Paired with violet gradients (135deg, violet → cyan) on featured surfaces.

### Neutral
- **Deep Void** (#0a0a14): Page background. The default state of the entire system.
- **Surface** (#0f0f1a): Cards, chat bubbles, glass panels. One step out of the void.
- **Surface Hover** (#1a1a28): Hover elevation for surfaces.
- **Border** (#1e1e30): Hairline borders on cards and inputs. Borders whisper, they don't outline.
- **Bone White** (#e8e8ed): Primary text.
- **Muted** (#8888a0): Secondary text, labels, meta. Blue-grey, never warm grey.

### Named Rules
**The Signal Rule.** Accents cover at most ~15% of any screen. Their rarity is the point — when violet appears, it means "act here."

**The Void Rule.** Backgrounds are always darker than surfaces, surfaces darker than borders, borders darker than text. The hierarchy is: void → surface → border → text. Never invert it.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Drystick-Bold (with Manrope, system-ui fallback)
**Body Font:** Noto Sans (with system-ui fallback)
**Label Font:** Noto Sans, uppercase, letterspaced (0.05–0.15em)

**Character:** Blocky, uppercase display type that takes over a viewport, paired with a quiet, clinical body face. The display is the personality; the body is the evidence.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, clamp(3.75rem→6.875rem), 0.9): Hero wordmarks (SILKDEV). Uppercase, tight tracking (-0.05em), user-select none. Appears once per page.
- **Headline** (700, clamp(2rem→2.5rem), 1.2): Section titles (What we're building, Full-service digital agency). Manrope, tight tracking (-0.03em).
- **Title** (700, clamp(1.75rem→2.125rem), 1.2): Card titles, philosophy quote (28–34px italic for the gold-rush quote).
- **Body** (400, 15–16px, 1.6–1.7): Paragraphs. Max line length ~65ch (max-w-lg/md containers).
- **Label** (600, 11–13px, 0.05–0.15em, uppercase): Section badges, nav links, footer headings, chip text.

### Named Rules
**The Uppercase Voice Rule.** Display and labels are uppercase; body copy is sentence case. The contrast between shout and whisper is the typographic personality.

## Layout

- **Container:** max-width 1152px (`max-w-6xl`), horizontal padding 40px.
- **Sections:** vertical rhythm of 60–100px padding (`py-[60px]` to `py-[100px]`), alternating near-black gradients (`hsla(0,0%,5%)` ↔ `hsla(260,30%,6%)`) so section boundaries read as subtle tonal shifts, not lines.
- **Grids:** 1 → 2 → 4 columns (products carousel), 1 → 2 (services), gap 16px.
- **Centering:** text and hero content center-aligned; body paragraphs capped at `max-w-md`/`max-w-lg` (~28rem/32rem).
- **Responsive:** display type scales with vw; hero CTAs stack under 640px; nav collapses to a compact bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Layered, not shadowed. The system conveys depth through tonal stacking (void → surface → surface-hover) and **glow blobs** — large blurred radial gradients (800px circles, `blur-[140px]`, accent at 5–25% opacity) placed behind hero and card content. One ambient shadow exists: a 20px violet glow (`0 0 20px rgba(108,99,255,0.15)`) for hover state on featured elements.

### Named Rules
**The No-Shadow Rule.** Cards and surfaces are flat at rest. Depth comes from tonal contrast and glow, not box shadows. The single violet glow is reserved for hover feedback.

## Shapes

- **Cards/containers:** gently curved, 16px (`rounded-2xl`). Product cards add a hover glow blob in their accent color at 15% opacity.
- **Buttons:** 10px radius (`rounded-[10px]`), pill-adjacent.
- **Chips/pills:** fully rounded (`rounded-full`), hairline border.
- **Inputs:** 12px (`rounded-xl`).
- **Chat bubbles:** 16px with one corner sharpened (4px) toward the sender — asymmetry as directionality.
- **Decorative geometry:** grid-pattern overlays (60px cells, 3–7% white), circular glow blobs, one curved decorative SVG path.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 10px radius, uppercase label type (11–14px, 600, tracked).
- **Primary:** Signal Violet background, white text, 12px 28px padding. Hover: opacity 90%. Active: scale 0.97 (btn-press).
- **Secondary:** transparent, hairline border, Bone White text. Hover: `bg-white/5`. Active: scale 0.97.
- **Featured:** violet → cyan gradient (135deg), white text, same press behavior.
- **Motion:** press feedback on all buttons via `transform: scale(0.97)` with `--ease-out` (cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)), 160ms.

### Chips
- **Style:** Surface background (50% alpha over void), hairline border, 11px uppercase label text, fully rounded, 6px 12px padding.
- **State:** static; used for badges (AI Intake, New project, Products, Services) and product status pills (LIVE, BETA, COMING SOON, IN DEVELOPMENT).

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px.
- **Background:** Surface (#0f0f1a); glass variant uses `rgba(15,15,26,0.6)` + 12px backdrop blur.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; hover glow blob in the card's accent color.
- **Border:** 1px hairline (#1e1e30).
- **Internal Padding:** 24px (p-6).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `bg-white/5`, hairline border (`border-white/10`), 12px radius, 10px 16px padding.
- **Focus:** border shifts to Signal Violet.
- **Placeholder:** white at 30% opacity.

### Navigation
- **Style:** uppercase tracked links (15px, 500, -0.01em), transparent background; active link gets violet text + `bg-[var(--accent)]/10`; hover lifts to white text. Compact bar, glass on scroll (`.nav-glass`, 16px blur).

### Signature: Custom Cursor
- A 6px violet dot (instant follow) + 36px ring (150ms ease-out follow). On interactive hover, the ring scales 1.33×, its border and fill tint violet at 40%/5% alpha. Transform-only animation — never width/height. On touch devices it simply doesn't render.

### Signature: Typing Indicator
- Three 6–8px muted dots, each animating `typingDot` 1.2s ease-out with 150ms stagger: a 4px upward lift with opacity 0.35→1. No bounce.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep backgrounds in the void family (#0a0a14–#1a1a28); a page should read as one continuous dark field.
- **Do** reserve Signal Violet for actionable/decision moments — buttons, active nav, focus.
- **Do** use uppercase for display and labels, sentence case for body.
- **Do** use glow blobs (blurred radial gradients) for depth; they're the system's shadow language.
- **Do** press everything interactive (scale 0.97) — the site reacts immediately or not at all.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add warm colors, gradients with gold/orange warmth, or pastel tints anywhere in the chrome (product cards keep their own identity accents).
- **Don't** use box shadows as primary elevation — tonal layering and glow only.
- **Don't** set body text below 14px or above 65–75ch line length.
- **Don't** uppercase body copy or long passages — uppercase is a display/label privilege.
- **Don't** animate width/height/left/top — transform and opacity only.
