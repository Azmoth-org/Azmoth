Running a company is hard. Running a modern, inclusive, sustainable, and authentic company is much harder.

One of the reasons I joined Pleo, was because it was all of those things. It was a great breath of fresh air. But not all companies are like that. All is not lost though, because companies can and do change, and you can help deliver that change.

So before you close off on plans for the winter holidays, and specifically December 9th 2021, note that  we’re running

Forward

, a digital summit for forward-thinkers at work. We have industry leaders and the “big thinkers” from all over to shed light on challenging topics that affect every aspect of the world of work.

And while “big thinkers” may be a bit vague, our roster currently includes:

📚 Kim Scott — Author of Radical Candor

🏡 Q Hamirani – Global Head of People Operations at Airbnb

📽 Sarah Manning — VP of People at Hopin

🌎 Ravin Jesuthasan, CFA, FRSA — Futurist, Author and Global Leader of Transformation at Merce

📺 Jessica Neal — Chief Talent Officer at Netflix

🎵 Marc McKenna-Coles — Diversity, Belonging and Inclusion Lead at Spotify

📖 Karen Tobiasen — Ex-CPO at Nordea and Phillips, and Author of the Heart Revolution

So, it’s a single day free summit where those leaders (and more) will shed light on challenging topics that affect every aspect of the world of work:

Diversity and inclusion

Mental health and resilience

Authentic leadership

International hiring

Founder failure stories

Sustainability
