June is usually a fairly quiet month. However, this June I participated in two trade-shows.

BDA Frankfurt by Whitehall Media was the first. It’s a very focused, one-day event that brings together data experts from Germany, Italy, France, the UK, Austria, Switzerland, and more.

I got the opportunity to speak at this event about the virtues of big data analytics in telecoms. Telecoms are notorious for being extremely competitive, with huge amounts of data, and very strict IT requirements. I showed how we used SQream DB to let these data-driven companies lead the way in customer engagement.

I’ve never before experienced clapping

during

a talk, but it seems the message resonates well!

A week later, I was off to Singapore for Communicasia 2019. This three-day event is held at the beautiful Marina Bay Sands expo center. While this event is primarily broadcast and network-equipment focused, the idea of analyzing more data to become even more data-driven is well-received by anyone who’s suffered the consequences of slow access to data.

The summer months usually don’t have too many shows lined-up, but we’re gearing up towards our next appearances at Strata 2019 in New York City and Tableau Conference 2019 in Las Vegas, with some exciting surprises.
